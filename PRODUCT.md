# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pump operators capture group attendance outdoors on mobile phones. Vendors review
their pumps and anonymous worker attendance. Administrators monitor platform-wide
attendance, operational health, reviews, and imports.

## Product Purpose

Face Attendance records morning and evening attendance from group photos,
auto-discovers pump-scoped anonymous identities, and gives authorized users clear
operational evidence without manual enrollment.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, dependable, clear.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a marketing landing page, decorative SaaS dashboard, dark
operations console, or playful consumer app. Avoid oversized metrics, nested
cards, gradients, glass effects, decorative illustrations, and unfamiliar
controls.

## Design Principles

1. Guide the next action.
2. Use evidence instead of decoration.
3. Support bright outdoor field conditions.
4. Keep anonymous identities honest and consistent.
5. Make review work efficient.
6. Use color only when it communicates action or state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, screen readers, 200 percent
zoom, reduced motion, high-contrast daylight use, touch targets of at least 44px,
and 48px targets on the pump capture flow.
